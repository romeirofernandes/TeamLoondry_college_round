# Handsfree

Handsfree combines deliberate pointing and speech for browsing in Chrome.

## Language

**Snapped target**:
The link, button, field, or other interactive page element selected near the pointer.
_Avoid_: guessed click destination

**Dwell**:
A continuous hold on one target long enough to indicate selection. Leaving the target cancels the hold.

**Fixed command**:
A known navigation or typing phrase with a defined action.

**Arbitrary find**:
A request to locate a page target described in the user's own words. Finding selects a target for review; it does not activate it.

**Tracking loss**:
The absence of a sufficiently reliable face or hand position. The last cursor position stays still.

**All tabs context**:
The titles of the user's open tabs available for choosing a tab by name.

**Dictation**:
Spoken words inserted as text into an editable field.

**Confirmation**:
A separate deliberate approval of a pending form submission or tab close.

**Broad hand hold**:
A steady hand position used to confirm a target without requiring a pinch.
